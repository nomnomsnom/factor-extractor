Live mode. This page is being served by serve.py, so it can fetch real quotes.

Paste bars from wherever you get them — CSV or tab-separated, with or without a header. A Symbol column covers many companies at once; without one, put the ticker in the box and paste a single company’s bars. Only the close is used, and only the latest one sets the multiples; earlier rows draw the line chart. Prices must be in the listing currency: US$ for the S&P names, S$ for the SGX ones. Nothing is sent anywhere — it stays in this browser, and on your account if sync is on. To fetch quotes instead of pasting them, run python3 serve.py from the project folder and open the address it prints — this page, published as an Artifact, is sandboxed and cannot make outside requests; served locally it can.

Where the numbers come from. Every figure in sections 2 and 4 — margins, cash flow, return on capital, debt, multiples and the five-year histories — was pulled from stockanalysis.com, which compiles company filings. The written answers in sections 1, 3 and 4 are research notes, not filings. Both can be wrong or out of date. Before you buy anything, check the primary document: the annual report and 10-K on SEC EDGAR for US listings, the SGX announcements page for Singapore listings. Every company below links to both.

This is not advice. It is a framework for doing your own work, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell. A company being large, profitable or well rated does not make it a good investment at today’s price. Brokerage and CDP accounts in Singapore generally require you to be 18.
